DW2PL RULES
1. Fights are to take place on the official Pro League small Fight Night cage, using the Official Pro League
game mode
Exceptions CAN be made, mostly for mobile players, and that will be treated on a case-by-case basis
2. One 20 minute round for title fights, 15 minute round for ranked matches, 10 minute round for
prelim/unranked matches.
3. The player with the most points at the end of the time wins. In the event of a tie, the player with more KOs
will win. If players have equal kills, HP will be the deciding factor. All player records/event results are
maintained within this document.
4. Same-region fights: Players must maintain a stable, REASONABLE connection for the duration of their fight.
Lag spikes may be addressed with pauses if the present admins deem it necessary, and they will invoke the
Lag Rule if they see fit.
Inter-Regional/mobile fights: Lag is to be expected; however, players with notoriously bad latency in any
region are not allowed to compete in Global. In SEVERE cases, the Lag Rule will be invoked.
Lag Rule: If players have lag issues during their match that would qualify for cancellation, they will have the
OPTION (both parties must agree) to proceed with the match under special rules; if the player with the
disruptive ping wins, the fight becomes a no contest (essentially an exhibition). If the player with the
disruptive ping loses, the fight will still count towards their rankings.
5. Turtling, running, spamming, and all other playstyles/tactics are ALLOWED. Although, freecam is not to be
used as a mechanic to assist striking.
6. BM (trash talk) is allowed. That being said, slurs and derogatory comments related to race, religion, etc. are
off limits and grounds for a ban. Fighters are also allowed to tune into the livestream during your own fight
and listen to the commentary if they so choose.
7. Santa Beards, Particles (including Yankee), Trails, Capes, hidden health bars, and visually dark presets that
hide player movement are all BANNED for the sake of performance and/or visual clarity.
8. Livestreaming, downloading games, videos, etc. or other actions that negatively affect player bandwidth are
prohibited during competition to keep the connection clean.
9. There will be no use of the in-game chat function from anyone except the admins officiating the fight, and
the two fighters themselves during competition.
10. All rules that apply outside of the Pro League in terms of conduct apply here as well.
11. Admin intervention in matches is up to our discretion, and our word is final.
